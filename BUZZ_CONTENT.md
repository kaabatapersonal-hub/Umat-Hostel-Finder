# Buzz launch content (Session 21, Hooks 1-3 & 5)

Ready-to-post content for the admin account, drafted ahead of Friday's
launch so posting is copy-paste, not composing-under-pressure. All of
these are meant to be posted as the admin account (auto-badged
"Official" the moment an admin's `author_id` posts — see
`protect_buzz_post_writes`), through the normal Buzz composer. Nothing
here is automated; every post below is something a human deliberately
pastes in and hits Post.

## 1. Launch day: the pinned welcome post

Post this first, then pin it (admin-only action, via the post's own
"..." menu → Pin). This is the very first thing anyone opens Buzz to.

> 🏠 Welcome to Campa Buzz — UMaT's housing community
>
> This is where UMaT students share hostel tips, post available rooms,
> find roommates, and ask questions about housing near campus.
>
> Drop a reply: **which hostel are you at or looking for?** We'll make
> sure it's listed on the platform 👇
>
> And if you're leaving campus this semester — watch this space.
> Leaving Campus Sale is coming to the Marketplace very soon.

**Why it works:** the "which hostel are you at" prompt drives the first
wave of replies (which double as free hostel-coverage data for you to
follow up on), and the Marketplace teaser plants a reason to check back
even for students who already have housing sorted.

## 2. Follow-up posts (use one every day or two after launch)

Post these as separate, un-pinned posts over the days following launch
— spacing them out keeps the feed feeling alive rather than dumping
everything at once. Reorder/skip based on what's actually happening
(e.g. don't post the "new hostels this week" one on a week nothing was
added).

1. **Water supply (drives replies + free data)**
   > Which area near UMaT has the best water supply? Drop your hostel
   > area below 👇

2. **Rooms filling up (drives hostel visits)** — only post this one
   about a hostel that's genuinely marked "Filling Up" or "Full" that
   day; see the reusable template in section 3 below for the real,
   per-hostel version of this idea.
   > Rooms filling up fast this week — check the app before your
   > favorite spot is gone 👀

3. **Roommate finding (makes Buzz useful beyond browsing)**
   > Looking for a roommate? Post here with your preferred hostel and
   > budget 🏠

4. **New hostels (drives feed visits)** — only post when actually true.
   > New hostels added this week — see what's new 🆕

5. **What-I-wish-I-knew (drives replies, builds a tips archive)**
   > What's one thing you wish you knew about your hostel before
   > moving in? Save the next person the stress 👇

6. **Priorities poll (low-effort reply, high engagement)**
   > Noise, water, security, distance to campus — what matters most
   > when picking a hostel? Reply with your top pick 💬

7. **Tag-a-friend (organic reach beyond people already using the app)**
   > Tag a coursemate who still hasn't found a hostel this semester 👀

## 3. Hostel Spotlight (reusable template, post every day or two)

Fill in the brackets from the hostel's actual current listing (price,
area, availability) before posting — never post a stale price. This is
free marketing for the hostel and gives the Buzz feed fresh, useful
content on a predictable cadence.

> 🏠 Hostel Spotlight: [Hostel Name] — [Area], from GHS [price]. Limited
> rooms available. See full details → [paste the hostel's /hostel/[id]
> link]

**Suggestion:** rotate spotlighted hostels rather than repeating the
same one — spreads the free exposure around and keeps the content from
feeling repetitive to regular readers.

## 4. Filling-up availability alert (reusable template, admin-approved, never automatic)

When you mark a hostel "Filling Up" or "Full" in the admin panel, use
this template to turn that status change into a Buzz post **if it's
genuinely newsworthy** (a popular hostel, a big price/room type) — this
is explicitly not something to post for every single status change,
just the ones worth surfacing.

> ⚠️ [Hostel Name] is filling up fast — [room type, e.g. "2 in a room"]
> still available from GHS [price]. Contact the manager directly 👇
> [WhatsApp link, e.g. https://wa.me/233XXXXXXXXX]

**Reminder:** this is a manual, admin-judgment call every time — never
wire this to fire automatically off an availability change. A hostel
manager quietly moving a listing back to "Available" a day later
shouldn't leave a stale "filling fast" post sitting in the feed.
