# Privacy Policy

**Campa — campagh.app**
**Last updated: July 27, 2026**

This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. We've written it in plain language because you deserve to understand it.

---

## Who We Are

Campa is operated by SimoForge, an independent product studio. Campa is not affiliated with or operated by the University of Mines and Technology (UMaT).

**Contact:** kaabatapersonal@gmail.com

## What We Collect

### Information you provide directly:

- **Account information:** your email address and a password when you create an account. Your display name if you set one.
- **Hostel reviews:** the text of your review, your rating, and which hostel it's about.
- **Buzz posts and replies:** the text content you post, and any GIFs you attach.
- **Marketplace listings:** item title, description, price, category, condition, photos you upload, and the WhatsApp number you provide for buyers to contact you.
- **Hostel submissions:** if you submit a hostel listing, the information you provide about that hostel including manager contact details and photos.
- **Saved hostels:** which hostels you save to your shortlist.

### Information collected automatically:

- **Basic usage data:** pages visited, features used, and general interaction patterns. We use this to understand how the platform is used and to improve it. We do not track individual browsing behavior for advertising.
- **Device information:** browser type, screen size, and operating system — used to ensure the platform works correctly on your device.

### Information we do NOT collect:

- We do not collect your phone number unless you voluntarily enter it (for marketplace listings or hostel submissions).
- We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or files unless you explicitly use a feature that requires it (such as uploading a photo or using the map's "locate me" feature).
- We do not track your location in the background. The map's "locate me" feature only works when you actively tap the button, and the location data is not stored.

## How We Use Your Information

- **To provide the service:** display your reviews, show your marketplace listings, publish your Buzz posts, save your shortlisted hostels, and enable contact between marketplace buyers/sellers and hostel managers.
- **To maintain and improve Campa:** understand which features are used, fix bugs, and improve the platform.
- **To moderate content:** review reports of abusive content, enforce community standards, and maintain the quality of listings and reviews.
- **To communicate with you:** send magic sign-in links to your email, and contact you regarding your account if necessary. We will not send marketing emails without your consent.

## What We Do NOT Do With Your Information

- **We do not sell your data.** Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. Ever.
- **We do not share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.**
- **We do not use your data for targeted advertising.** Campa does not display ads.
- **We do not share your email address publicly.** Your email is used for account authentication only and is never displayed to other users.

## Who Can See Your Information

- **Your display name:** visible on your reviews, Buzz posts, and marketplace listings.
- **Your reviews:** visible to all users on the hostel you reviewed.
- **Your Buzz posts and replies:** visible to all users.
- **Your marketplace listings:** visible to all users, including the WhatsApp number you provide for contact. Note: when you list an item, you are choosing to make your WhatsApp number public on that listing.
- **Your saved hostels:** visible only to you.
- **Your email address:** visible only to you and Campa administrators. Never shared publicly.

## Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a hosted database platform, with servers protected by industry-standard security measures.
- We use row-level security policies to ensure users can only access data they are authorized to see.
- Photos you upload are compressed and stored securely in cloud storage.
- We run automated security audits regularly to identify and fix vulnerabilities.
- Despite our efforts, no system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Campa:

- **Supabase** (database and authentication) — [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Vercel** (hosting) — [vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Klipy** (GIF search in Buzz) — when you search for GIFs, your search query is sent to Klipy's servers to return results. Klipy's privacy policy applies to this data.
- **Resend** (email delivery for sign-in links) — [resend.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy)

We do not share your personal data with these services beyond what is necessary for them to provide their function (e.g., Supabase stores your account data, Resend delivers your sign-in email).

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access your data:** you can view your account information, reviews, listings, and posts at any time through the platform.
- **Correct your data:** you can edit your profile, reviews, and marketplace listings at any time.
- **Delete your content:** you can delete your marketplace listings, Buzz posts, and Buzz replies at any time. Deleted content is removed from the platform.
- **Request account deletion:** contact us at kaabatapersonal@gmail.com and we will delete your account and associated data. Note that reviews and posts you made may be retained in anonymized form (with your name removed) to maintain the integrity of hostel information.
- **Request your data:** contact us and we will provide you with a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

## Children

Campa is designed for university students. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years old. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

## Cookies

Campa uses essential cookies for authentication (keeping you signed in). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies that identify individual users.

## Data Retention

- Your account data is retained as long as your account exists.
- Marketplace listings marked as "sold" remain visible for a period before being archived.
- Deleted content is removed from the platform. Backups may retain data for a limited period for disaster recovery purposes.
- If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
